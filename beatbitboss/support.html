Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I restore my "Full Game" purchase?

If you reinstalled BeatBitBoss or switched to a new iPhone/iPad, simply tap on any locked boss card to open the purchase window, then tap "Restore Purchases". Apple will securely verify your Apple ID and instantly unlock all 30 bosses at no charge.

Q. Which content is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

The first 3 bosses (Moonlight, Dreamweaver, and Wind) are 100% free to play with no time limits. The one-time Non-Consumable In-App Purchase "Full Game" ($2.99) permanently unlocks all remaining 27 bosses, 4-lane challenge modes, and the offline Jukebox (My List). There are no recurrent subscriptions or consumable coins.

Q. How do I calibrate audio latency?

Bluetooth headphones (like AirPods) have natural wireless delay. During any boss fight, tap the pause button (II) in the top-right corner. You can adjust audio latency with the + / - buttons in 10 ms increments to achieve flawless rhythm sync.

Q. Does BeatBitBoss require an internet connection?

No! BeatBitBoss works 100% offline. All boss charts, visual effects, and retro soundtrack files are stored entirely within the app package. You can play on airplanes, subways, or offline mode without limitations.

Q. How does Family Sharing work?

BeatBitBoss supports Apple Family Sharing! Once you purchase "Full Game", up to 5 family members on your iCloud Family account can access and play all 30 bosses on their own iOS devices without purchasing again.

Q. Where is my game progress saved?

Your high scores, boss victories, and combo records are saved directly in local storage on your device. We do not track or store your data on external cloud servers, guaranteeing absolute privacy.

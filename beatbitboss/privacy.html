Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Big Mustache Games

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

BeatBitBoss ("we", "us", or "our") is designed to deliver a pure, distraction-free rhythm arcade experience. We strongly believe in user privacy and practice Zero Data Collection.

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, device identifiers, or biometric information.

2. What Data We Collect

Absolutely None.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, IP address, or location.
  • No Tracking SDKs: There are no third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Firebase, Meta Pixel, or AppsFlyer) embedded in BeatBitBoss.
  • No Advertisements: BeatBitBoss contains zero third-party advertisement networks or ad trackers.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

Your high scores, unlocked boss stages, audio latency preferences, and custom volume levels are saved purely locally on your Apple device using standard sandboxed storage.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you delete the application from your device.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

All in-app purchases ("Full Game") are processed entirely by Apple via the official App Store and StoreKit API.

We never receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank account details. Apple handles payment processing according to the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

BeatBitBoss is safe for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13, in compliance with COPPA and GDPR.

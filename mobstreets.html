Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mob Streets an offline game?

Yes. Mob Streets is 100% offline. No cellular data or Wi-Fi connection is required after downloading the game from the App Store.

Are there any In-App Purchases, ads, or subscriptions?

No. Mob Streets is an upfront paid premium title ($2.99). It contains zero in-app purchases, zero microtransactions, and zero advertisements. All cars, weapons, military tanks, and district missions are unlocked entirely through gameplay.

How do controls work on iPhone and iPad?

Use the virtual thumbstick on the left side of the screen to move and steer. Use the dedicated action buttons on the right side to Fire weapons, Enter/Exit vehicles, Brake/Jump, and switch firearms. Tap the MAP button in the top right corner to expand the city map, and tap the X button in the corner to close it.

Where is my saved game stored?

Your save file and game progression are stored exclusively on your device inside the local iOS sandbox (user://). If you delete the app, your local save data is removed by iOS.

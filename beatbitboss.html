Frequently Asked Questions

How do in-app purchases work?

The first 3 bosses are free to play forever. You can unlock all 30 bosses with a single one-time In-App Purchase ("Full Game"). There are no subscriptions, timers, or consumable coins.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstall BeatBitBoss or switch devices, tap on any locked boss to open the unlock modal and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple will verify your previous purchase and unlock all bosses for free.

Does the game require internet?

No, BeatBitBoss works 100% offline. All music, visuals, and game logic are bundled directly in the app.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

BeatBitBoss ("we", "our") is designed with your privacy in mind. Our privacy practices are simple and transparent:

  • No Data Collection: BeatBitBoss does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, identifiers, device IDs, or usage metrics.
  • Local Storage Only: Your game progress, unlocked bosses, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to external servers and are removed if you delete the app.
  • No Advertisements or Analytics: The app contains no third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or analytics services.

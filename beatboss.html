Frequently Asked Questions

Is BeatBitBoss an offline game?

Yes. BeatBitBoss is 100% offline. All boss music tracks, visual effects, and retro charts are stored directly on your device. No internet connection is required after downloading the game from the App Store.

How do In-App Purchases and boss unlocks work?

The first 3 bosses are completely free to play forever. A single one-time Non-Consumable In-App Purchase ("Full Game" for $2.99) permanently unlocks all remaining 27 bosses. There are zero subscriptions, zero consumable currency, and zero advertisements.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstall the game or switch iOS devices, tap on any locked boss card to open the purchase prompt, then tap "Restore Purchases" (Satın Alımı Geri Yükle). Apple will verify your Apple ID and instantly restore access to all bosses without charging you again.

How does Family Sharing work?

BeatBitBoss supports Apple Family Sharing. When you purchase "Full Game", up to 5 family members on your iCloud Family account can access and play all bosses on their iOS devices without purchasing again.

How do I adjust audio latency for AirPods / Bluetooth?

Bluetooth headphones introduce wireless latency. During any boss fight, tap the pause button (II) in the top right corner. You can adjust audio latency using the + / - buttons (in 10 ms increments) to achieve perfect rhythm sync.

Where is my saved game data stored?

Your high scores, combo records, and unlocked bosses are stored locally on your device in the iOS sandbox. No personal data is ever uploaded to external servers.
